Our Mission

Culture is not content.
It is identity.

Mainstream platforms were not built for your language, your traditions, or your community. Luvart was.

We built a home for the stories that matter — the ones passed down through generations, the ones that exist only in the languages they were born in, the ones that define who we are and who we become through each other.

In a world that forgets
Every story shared, every indigenous language audio recorded, every tradition documented on Luvart is an act of preservation. Your culture deserves more than a hashtag.
Luvart connects
No ads. No data selling. No third-party tracking. Just culture, given a home worthy of it.

What You Can Do

A platform built for the culture

01
Cultural Stories
Share photos, videos, and stories from your real cultural life — traditional clothing, music, art, ceremonies, food, and the everyday moments that define your community. Not filtered. Not sanitised. Real.
02
Cultural Circles
Communities built around heritage, language, and shared tradition. Find your people. Welcome others. Grow together in spaces that honour who you are.
03
Culture Exchange Feed
Discover living traditions from every corner of the world. Filter by language, theme, or location. Curiosity without borders. Connection without translation.
04
Indigenous Language Preservation
Your mother tongue belongs on the internet. Record songs, greetings, proverbs, and stories. Build a living archive of languages that mainstream platforms have never made space for.

Coming After Luvart

The knowledge that was always there

A child in Venda who sees a bird and wants to know more is forced to know its English name or receive nothing useful. A student in Limpopo researching their clan's history must think in English to retrieve knowledge that was never English to begin with.

The knowledge exists. The retrieval system was built for a different human.

Setso changes that. Search as you are. In the language you think in.

The Company

THE LUVART PLATFORMS (PTY) LTD

A South African technology company building cultural digital infrastructure for Africa and the world. Founded on a single belief — that African communities, and cultural communities everywhere, deserve digital spaces that treat their identity as worthy of preservation rather than monetisation.

01
Mission-Alignment is Non-Negotiable
Every decision must feel like a natural extension of the platform's purpose. No exploitative ads. No compromises.
02
Community-First
We monetise around the community, not from it. Trust is the most valuable asset we have.
03
Data Sovereignty
Your stories belong to you. Your community's knowledge belongs to your community. We are a vessel, not an owner.
04
Ubuntu by Design
I am because we are. This is not a tagline. It is the architectural principle behind every product decision we make.
