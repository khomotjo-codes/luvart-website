Our Mission

Culture is not content.
It is identity.

Mainstream platforms were built around one default — one language to think in, one beauty standard, one idea of normal — and everyone else's culture gets treated as a niche add-on. That's not an accident. That's a hundred years of media built to flatten difference into one exportable, sellable version of culture.

Luvart is a refusal of that default. It's a place to think, post, and speak first in your own tongue — not the one you were taught was the "real" one. To argue with your own community, in your own words, without performing for an audience that was never the point. This is identity reclamation, built into the product, not a value statement bolted onto it.

Decolonising the feed
Every post in your mother tongue, every argument that doesn't get flattened for a global audience, every joke that doesn't need a footnote — that's the algorithm working for you instead of erasing you. Your culture was never the niche. It was always the point.
Luvart connects
No ads. No data selling. No third-party tracking. Just culture, talking to itself and to the world, on its own terms.

What You Can Do

A platform built for the culture

01
Cultural Stories
Post what's actually happening — what you're cooking tonight, what you're wearing to the wedding this weekend, the song stuck in your head, the take you want your community's reaction to. Traditional clothing and ceremonies belong here too, but so does the ordinary Tuesday. Not filtered. Not sanitised. Not explained for outsiders.
Live feed, not an exhibit
02
Cultural Circles
Spaces built around heritage, language, and shared tradition — where the conversation never stops. Ask a question and get an answer in hours, not years. Debate something with your own people. Welcome someone learning. This is where belonging happens in real time, not just where it's declared.
Always something being discussed
03
Culture Exchange Feed
Discover what other communities are living right now — not a curated, translated, watered-down version, the real thing, in the language it happens in. Filter by language, theme, or location. Curiosity without borders. Connection without flattening anyone to make it palatable.
Updates daily, not archived
04
Mother Tongue, First
Speak, post, and record in the language you actually think in — songs, greetings, proverbs, arguments, voice notes to your group chat. Every recording becomes part of a living language that mainstream platforms never made space for, but you're not making it for an archive. You're making it because that's how you talk.
Used daily, not displayed

Coming After Luvart

The knowledge being made every day

A child forced to know a bird's English name just to learn about it. A student who must think in a second language to retrieve knowledge that was never in that language to begin with. A speaker of Welsh, Tamil, Quechua, or Yoruba who finds the internet has no place for the language they dream in.

This isn't a gap in translation. It's a hundred years of search and knowledge systems built around one language and calling it universal.

Setso changes that. Search as you are. In the language you think in. Powered by what communities on Luvart are sharing right now, so the answers grow as the conversation does.

The Company

THE LUVART PLATFORMS (PTY) LTD

A technology company building cultural digital infrastructure for every community on earth. Founded on a single belief — that difference is not something platforms should flatten in pursuit of scale, but the thing worth building scale around.
