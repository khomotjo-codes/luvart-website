Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 18 August 2024  ·  Last Updated: 9 March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Luvart Platforms (Pty) Ltd ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, protects, and handles your personal information when you use Luvart.

Luvart is operated by The Luvart Platforms (Pty) Ltd, a company registered in South Africa. By using Luvart, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Our Privacy Commitment

Luvart is an ad-free platform built on the philosophy of Ubuntu — the belief that your identity and your heritage are not products to be sold.

We make the following commitments to every user:

  • We do not sell your data. We do not sell, rent, trade, or broker your personal information to any third party.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking. Luvart does not integrate advertising networks, marketing pixels, or third-party analytics services such as Google AdMob, Firebase, or Meta Pixel.
  • We do not use cookies. Luvart uses Redis-backed sessions and JSON Web Tokens (JWTs) for authentication. No tracking cookies are placed on your device.
  • We control our own infrastructure. Your data is stored on servers operated directly by The Luvart Platforms (Pty) Ltd. We do not rely on third-party backend-as-a-service providers.

2. The Law That Protects You

We are committed to compliance with applicable data protection law across all markets where Luvart operates.

Africa (Primary) — Our primary legal framework is the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa. As we expand across the continent, we comply with applicable national data protection legislation in each African market.

India — We comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

Mexico — We comply with Mexico's Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares (LFPDPPP).

Indonesia — We comply with Indonesia's Government Regulation No. 71 of 2019 and applicable personal data protection obligations.

If you are located in a country not listed above, we apply the standards of POPIA as a baseline minimum for all users globally.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide a secure, functional, and culturally meaningful platform.

3.1 Account Data

When you create an account, we collect: name and username, email address, phone number (if provided), date of birth (to verify minimum age eligibility), and profile photo (if uploaded).

3.2 Content You Share

We store content you voluntarily create and share, including photos, videos, written posts, audio recordings, comments, reactions, and Cultural Circle contributions.

3.3 Technical and Security Data

To maintain platform security and stability, we collect IP addresses (pseudonymised in logs), device type, operating system version, app version, and session activity data managed via server-side Redis sessions — not browser cookies. This data is used strictly for security monitoring, fraud detection, and platform stability. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

3.4 Location Data

We collect location data only when you explicitly grant permission to enable specific features such as LuvMap. We collect approximate (coarse) location, not precise GPS coordinates, unless you choose to share a specific location with a post. Location data is not collected in the background and is not used for advertising.

3.5 What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not place cookies on your device
  • We do not collect biometric data
  • We do not collect data from your contacts, camera, or microphone without your explicit in-app permission for a specific action
  • We do not collect data about your activity on other platforms or websites

4. Cultural Data Sovereignty

Luvart recognises that cultural content — stories, indigenous language recordings, traditional knowledge, and oral heritage — carries community ownership, historical significance, and identity.

Your cultural content belongs to you and your community.

  • You retain ownership of the cultural knowledge you share on Luvart.
  • Luvart acts as a secure vessel for this information, not an owner of it.
  • Your cultural content and voice recordings are not used to train external artificial intelligence systems or large language models without your explicit informed consent.
  • Indigenous language audio recordings are governed by your individual and community consent settings. You may withdraw consent at any time.

6. Technical Safeguards

Encryption in transit — All communication between the Luvart app and our servers is protected using TLS 1.3.

Encryption at rest — Sensitive personal data fields are encrypted at the column level using AES-GCM-SIV before being written to our servers.

Key management — Encryption keys are managed through a dedicated secure key management system and are never stored alongside the data they protect.

Authentication — Luvart uses JSON Web Tokens (JWTs) for authentication and server-side Redis sessions for session management. We do not use browser cookies.

No cookies — Luvart does not place any cookies — tracking, functional, or otherwise — on your device.

7. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share limited data with third parties only in the following circumstances: legal obligations, imminent safety concerns, and infrastructure providers who process data on our behalf under contractual data processing agreements.

We do not share data with advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing platforms under any circumstances.

8. International Data Transfers

The Luvart Platforms (Pty) Ltd is headquartered in South Africa. Our primary servers serving African users are located in South Africa, ensuring that African user data remains on the continent. Any cross-border transfer of personal data is conducted in accordance with the requirements of POPIA and applicable local law.
